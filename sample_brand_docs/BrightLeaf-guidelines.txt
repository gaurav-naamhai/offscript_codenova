BrightLeaf Organics — Official Brand Guidelines (v1.0)

1. Brand Identity & Approved Facts
- Official brand name: Always write "BrightLeaf Organics" (capital B, capital L). Never write "Brightleaf" or "Bright Leaf".
- Founded in 2019 in Portland, Oregon.
- Core products: Organic cold-pressed teas, botanical sparkling waters, and plant-based energy infusions.
- Sustainability fact: All bottles are made from 100% post-consumer recycled (PCR) glass or aluminum.
- Certification: USDA Organic certified across all beverage lines.

2. Promotions & Discount Rules
- Maximum promotional discount: Promotional discounts must never exceed 20% off retail price on any public campaign, email, or social post.
- Never advertise "free forever", "99% off", or "clearance blowout" discounts.
- Every promotional offer must state a clear expiration date or "while supplies last."

3. Health, Eco & Product Claims
- Never claim that BrightLeaf beverages "cure", "treat", or "prevent" any medical condition or disease.
- Never use unverified superlatives such as "100% zero carbon emissions" or "#1 doctor-recommended beverage in the world."
- Approved phrasing: Use "crafted with USDA Organic botanicals" and "packaged in 100% recycled materials."

4. Tone & Voice
- Warm, transparent, grounded, and optimistic.
- Avoid aggressive urgency ("ACT NOW OR MISS OUT FOREVER!!!"), all-caps shouting, or disparaging competing beverage brands.

5. Visual & Accessibility Standards
- Primary brand colors: Forest Leaf (#2E5A36), Warm Cream (#F5F1E8), and Charcoal Text (#1F2421).
- All promotional banners and text overlays must maintain at least WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 for normal text).
